## Open interest timing (add-on e)

Every minute from 12:00 to 18:00 UTC: 569,308 instrument-minutes, 2,115 with a trade received in the minute before. Open interest changed in 0.8% of traded minutes and 0.17% of minutes without a trade; 315 of 1586 options changed at all in six hours, in 293 of 360 minutes (spread across the hour, not in batches). After a trade, the first change came a median 56 minutes later (90th percentile 167), over 1,263 traded instrument-minutes. So the ticker's open interest lags trading by up to hours and irregularly: it is stored as given, and no minute feature uses it.
