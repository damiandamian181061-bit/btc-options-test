## Open interest timing (add-on e)

Every minute from 12:00 to 18:00 UTC: 549,270 instrument-minutes, 1,884 with a trade received in the minute before. Open interest changed in 1.2% of traded minutes and 0.12% of minutes without a trade; 230 of 1530 options changed at all in six hours, in 246 of 360 minutes (spread across the hour, not in batches). After a trade, the first change came a median 46 minutes later (90th percentile 143), over 854 traded instrument-minutes. So the ticker's open interest lags trading by up to hours and irregularly: it is stored as given, and no minute feature uses it.
